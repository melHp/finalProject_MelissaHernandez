# Title
Cozy Fishing Minigame

## Repository
https://github.com/melHp/finalProject_MelissaHernandez.git

## Description
A cozy, colorful fishing minigame, where the character is stationary (sitting by a lake) and the fishing mechanics are point-and-click based. The fish caught will be added to a collection journal where the player can keep track of fishing progress.

## Features
- Fishing mechanics
	- Players will be able to click on a fishable spot in a lake to cast a fishing line. After a randomized (not too long) period of time, a fish bites, giving the player the opportunity to click again and catch it. 
- Different fish types
	- The game will include multiple types of fish with different appearances and rarities. Fish will be selected randomly when the player catches one. 
- Fish collection jurnal
	- Players will be able to see the type of fish they have caught so far, read information about them, and track their collection progress.
- Visuals and Animations
    - The game will feature a colorful environment. Sprites for stationary character and fish will be needed. Animation will be kept at a minimum for the fishing line and small effects. 

## Challenges
- Learning how to handle mouse inputs. 
- Implementing time-based events.
- Manage different stages of fishing.
- Learning to display and update sprites and visuals in Python.
- Organizing fish data in player collection.

## Outcomes
Ideal Outcome:
- A polished-looking fishing minigame with working fishing mechanics, has multiple fish types with different rarities, a collection journal with information, and simple animations. 

Minimal Viable Outcome:
- A functional fishing minigame where players can cast their fishing line, catch random fish, and view their catches in a basic collection menu.
